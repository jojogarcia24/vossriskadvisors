When a quote closes and the carrier issues the policy, click Policy issued. A task appears about 50 days before each renewal, due 30 days out, so you can re-quote before the client's renewal email (45 days out). When you're done, pick the outcome: the policy rolls into its next term (or is marked lost) and the next renewal reminder is scheduled automatically.

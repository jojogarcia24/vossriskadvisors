# Commercial Appetite — Quick Reference

> Voss Risk Advisors places **commercial lines** through **Smart Choice's Smart Start Commercial (SSCL)** program.
> This gives us access to a bench of A-rated national carriers **without per-carrier appointments**.
> Framing: we are *backed by A-rated national carriers* — not "directly appointed with" them.
> Program executed via Combined Addendum 10/22/2026. Agency TX General Lines P&C license 3514065.

**Only say yes to what's in the appetite below.** If a class/line doesn't appear for the risk in the
Smart Start platform, it's outside the online appetite — check with the Smart Start team before declining.
Never quote specific prices, premiums, or bind-time guarantees.
